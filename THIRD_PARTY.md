# Third-party assets

## MakeHuman / MPFB study figure

Skem's `prototype/asset-first-v03` branch loads the `man.glb` study figure generated with MakeHuman / MPFB 2 and bundled by the open-source `kunalkushwaha/vsim` project.

Source: `https://github.com/kunalkushwaha/vsim/tree/main/packages/assets/library`

The vsim asset credits identify `human.glb`, `man.glb`, `kid.glb`, and `suited.glb` as MakeHuman / MPFB-generated outputs released under CC0 (public domain). The MakeHuman project likewise states that its bundled assets are CC0 and that generated outputs may be used without restriction.

This asset is being used as a prototype foundation only. Skem should validate drawing-relevant anatomical landmarks and deformation quality before describing any model as teaching-grade anatomy.
